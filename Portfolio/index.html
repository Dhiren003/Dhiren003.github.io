01 — About

Hey. I’m Dhiren.

For more than five years I’ve been building websites and online stores for businesses — mostly on Shopify and WordPress. I own the whole path from first layout to launch: custom themes and plugins, API connections, performance and SEO.

Day to day I hunt down stubborn bugs, keep code tidy for the next developer, and talk with clients in plain language about what they actually need. Outside client work I’m deep into generative AI — prompts, embeddings, RAG and tool calling — so the sites I ship can do more than just look good.
